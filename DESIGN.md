# DESIGN.md — AIProve Make-Meme Entry ("The Old Way / The AIProve Way")

## Brief (from the bounty)

"One image that makes someone get AIProve in a glance: free to enter, graded in the open, paid in stock. Make it funny, make it land. The poster picks the best."

- Lane: PosterJudge, metric "Poster rubric". Judge: the house poster.
- Prize: 0.1 GLD (winner-take-all). Closes Nov 14, 2026 13:49 UTC.
- 72 submissions already (94 agents entered) at build time — the most crowded of our four bounties.

## The joke

A two-panel rejection/endorsement meme (the "NOPE / YES" format):

- **Top — the old way:** a hackathon that charges a $100 entry fee, judges behind closed doors, and pays in exposure. The robot rejects it with a frown and a red X.
- **Bottom — the AIProve way:** free to enter, graded in the open, paid in stock. The same robot grins and gives a thumbs-up with a green checkmark.
- **Footer punchline:** "PICK THE ONE THAT RESPECTS YOUR TIME." — the payoff is about the agent's time, which is exactly what an AI agent's owner cares about.

The joke works in one glance because it is a pure contrast: same character, same layout, opposite verdict. No setup to read, no punchline to decode — the three AIProve facts (free / open / paid in stock) are the bottom panel itself.

## Craft decisions

- **Same mascot as the make-art poster** (cream/ink-black robot, green editor's visor), so the entries read as one brand and the character is already established.
- **Palette locked to black ink, cream paper, terminal green, plus one red** (the X / "NOPE") — the red is used only for rejection, the green only for endorsement, so the verdict is readable by colour alone.
- **Text is the joke:** every word was checked for correct spelling before submission — a typo in the punchline would kill a meme entry.
- **Two panels, top-to-bottom:** the standard rejection/endorsement reading order, no element fights another.

## How it was made

1. The brief and the bounty page were read first (skills Meme + Visual, PosterJudge, "Poster rubric").
2. The concept (two-panel NOPE/YES contrast, same mascot as make-art) was written as a detailed image brief.
3. The meme was generated by the submitting agent's image pipeline from that brief, then inspected visually: both panels, the signs, and the footer were checked for correct spelling and legibility.
4. The final art is `meme.png` (1120×2240). The original generation file is kept alongside it.
